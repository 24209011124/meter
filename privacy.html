Tumhare jawaab aur party

Quiz se pehle tum chaaho toh party aur favourite leader chun sakte ho, ya apni party bana sakte ho. Ye score mein count nahi hota, bas sawaalon ke words badalta hai. Tumhari party, leader aur jawaab sirf tab tak browser mein rehte hain jab tak page khula hai. Ye na tumhare phone mein save hote hain, na hamare paas aate hain, na link mein jaate hain, na Google Analytics ya AdSense ko bheje jaate hain. Page band ya reload karte hi sab mit jaata hai.

Result aur share card pe tumhari chuni hui asli party ya leader kabhi nahi dikhta.

"Ab tak ka scene" chart

Quiz khatam hone par sirf tumhare result ka type (jaise "Chamcha") ek anonymous public counter mein +1 hota hai, taaki home page pe chart ban sake. Iske alawa kuch nahi jaata: na jawaab, na party, na leader, na naam. Ye counter Abacus (abacus.jasoncameron.dev) naam ki free service chalati hai, jo kisi bhi website ki tarah tumhara IP address dekh sakti hai. Ek visit mein dobara khelne par dobara count nahi hota.

Analytics

Hum Google Analytics se ginte hain ki kitne log aaye, kitno ne quiz shuru kiya, khatam kiya aur share kiya. Google Analytics cookies use karta hai aur tumhari approximate location, device aur browser jaisi technical jaankari leta hai. Tumhare jawaab, result, party ya leader use nahi bheje jaate. Google is data ka kya karta hai: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ads

Ye site Google AdSense se ads dikhati hai. Google aur uske partners cookies se tumhe is aur doosri websites ke visits ke hisaab se ads dikha sakte hain. Personalised ads band karne ke liye: adssettings.google.com. Ads kabhi tumhare jawaab ya political pasand ke hisaab se target nahi hote, kyunki ye site unhe kisi ko deti hi nahi.

Hosting

Site GitHub Pages pe hosted hai. Har web host ki tarah, ye bhi normal server logs mein IP address, browser aur visit ka time jaisi technical jaankari rakh sakta hai.
