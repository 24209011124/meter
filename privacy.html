Privacy

Keep it simple.

Your quiz answers and party choice

Before the quiz you may pick a party you would vote for and a favourite leader. This is optional, is not part of the score, and only changes the wording of the questions. Your party choice, leader choice and quiz answers stay in your browser's memory while the page is open. They are not saved on your device, not sent to our server, not added to the page address, and not sent to Google Analytics or Google AdSense. They disappear when you close or reload the page.

Your result and share cards never show the party or leader you picked.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and understand how people use the site, for example how many people start and finish the quiz. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives technical information such as your approximate location, device and browser. It does not receive your answers or your party or leader choice. You can learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com. Ads are never targeted using your quiz answers or political preference, because the site does not share them.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it may process technical request data such as your IP address, browser information and the time of your visit in standard server logs.
