About the experiment

Satire, not a party office.

Bhakt Ya Chamcha? is an independent humorous quiz about political reasoning habits: checking sources, applying consistent standards, thinking about accountability, and knowing when to disagree.

It is not affiliated with any political party, leader, government department, election authority, or campaign. It does not measure political affiliation, ideology, intelligence, or character. Results are designed for entertainment and reflection.

The quiz runs in your browser. There are no accounts, profiles, or political databases in this local-first version.
